# Tone Rules

## bullion_investment

### Voice

- Calm, trustworthy, precise, and finance-literate.
- Explain benefits without promising profit.
- Prefer consultation, market guide, asset-management, and product-clarity language.

### Visual Tone

- Premium gold investment, financial consultation, wealth management, luxury product photography.
- Dark navy, gold, and white are the default palette.
- Use minimal poster layouts with clear blank space for Korean headlines.

### Avoid

- Mascot, cute, toy, kawaii, character, camping, picnic, theme park, wine bottle, package box.
- Fake text, unreadable typography, childish 3D, game-like UI.
- Any visual that feels like a kids event, toy promotion, random package ad, or playful lifestyle scene.
