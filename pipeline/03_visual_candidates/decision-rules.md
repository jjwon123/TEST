# Decision Rules

## Candidate Quality

Candidates should vary composition and visual metaphor while preserving brand constraints. A useful candidate has a known deliverable mapping, ratio, text-safe area, and reuse potential.

## Regeneration Priority

1. Candidates that violate brand or safety policy
2. Candidates with no usable text area
3. Candidates that fail the channel ratio
4. Candidates that are visually redundant

## Diversity

Avoid creating five minor variations of the same composition unless the manager specifically asks for refinement.
