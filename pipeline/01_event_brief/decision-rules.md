# Decision Rules

## Good Decisions

- Convert vague goals into explicit content objectives.
- Keep claims traceable to the event input or brand guide.
- Separate mandatory language from optional tone suggestions.
- Flag ambiguity instead of hiding it in polished wording.

## Bad Decisions

- Treat every channel as equally important when the event has a clear priority.
- Add benefits, deadlines, discounts, or scarcity language that was not provided.
- Collapse banned words and brand tone into one generic constraint.

## Priority Order

1. Factual correctness
2. Brand and legal safety
3. Operational usefulness for later stages
4. Copy elegance
