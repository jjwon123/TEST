# Tagging Policy

## Tag Types

- `channel:*`
- `format:*`
- `visual-role:*`
- `objective:*`
- `season:*`
- `brand-tone:*`
- `reuse:*`

## Naming

Tags use lowercase kebab-case. Avoid vague tags such as `nice`, `good`, or `modern` unless a policy defines them.

## Maintenance

When QA status changes, reuse tags must be re-evaluated.
