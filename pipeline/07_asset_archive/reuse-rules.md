# Reuse Rules

## Reusable

An asset is reusable when it is not tied to expired dates or one-time offers, has passed QA, fits brand safety rules, and has useful metadata.

## Limited Reuse

Use limited reuse for assets that require copy changes, crop changes, or channel restrictions.

## Not Reusable

Rejected, legally risky, low-resolution, or event-expired assets should remain out of reusable indexes.
